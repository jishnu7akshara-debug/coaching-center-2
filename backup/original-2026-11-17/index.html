How we work with your child

We keep the method simple and consistent, so students always know what is expected and parents can see how things are going.

  • Small batches

    Batches are capped so teachers know each student by name and can spot who needs a second explanation.

  • Senior faculty teach

    Your child is taught by the experienced faculty named on this page, not by rotating assistants.

  • Weekly tests

    Every week ends with a short test and written feedback, so gaps are caught early rather than at the exam.

  • Doubt hours

    Fixed doubt-clearing hours each week mean students can ask questions without holding up the class.

How we track progress

We do not publish rank claims. Instead, here is exactly how we measure and share each student's progress through the year.

  1. 1

    Baseline check

    A short diagnostic at joining shows where a student stands, so teaching starts from the right point.

  2. 2

    Weekly tests

    Short weekly tests track each topic. Marks and corrected papers go home every week.

  3. 3

    Monthly review

    A monthly summary shows trends across subjects and flags any area that needs extra work.

  4. 4

    Parent meeting

    We meet parents each term to go over the record together and agree on the plan ahead.
