Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Sri Saraswathi Academy ("we", "us") runs this website. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We only receive information you choose to send us:

  • When you use the enquiry form, your name, mobile number, chosen program and any message are placed into a WhatsApp message. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp, and the form data is not stored on this website.
  • When you call, email or message us directly, we receive the details you share in that conversation.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a counselling session.
  • For enrolled students, to share test results, attendance and progress updates with the student and parents.
  • To send fee receipts and important notices about classes.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Children's data

Many of our students are under 18. We collect a student's details only with the consent of a parent or guardian, and we share progress information only with the student and their parents or guardians.

Third parties

Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for up to 12 months if you do not enrol. Records for enrolled students are kept for the academic year and up to three years after, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you or your child, or withdraw consent at any time. Write to hello@saraswathiacademy.example or call +91 90000 00000, and we will respond within 30 days.
